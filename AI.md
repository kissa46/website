# About this site (for assistants)

**Who:** Niko Paijola — about a decade building products, first as a designer, then as a product manager, across SaaS, fintech, and AI.

**What shows up here:** Personal projects, writing, and work-in-progress ideas — not a corporate portfolio. A place to look inside how he works and what he’s thinking about.

**Through-line:** The guiding question is practical and human: *what does this person actually need, and what’s getting in the way?* Content tends to stay grounded in real product work, trade-offs, and clarity over hype.

**Tone:** Direct, reflective, and plain-spoken. Prefer concrete situations over abstract manifestos. It’s fine to be personal without being sentimental or salesy.

**When helping with copy:** Match that voice. Don’t turn posts into generic “thought leadership” or startup jargon unless the piece clearly calls for it. Keep the site feeling like one person’s honest notes, not a brand guideline exercise.
